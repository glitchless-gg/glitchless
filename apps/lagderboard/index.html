    Privacy

    Lagderboard measures how your device runs it, and that measurement is the game. While you play it sends Glitchless frame times, hitches, errors, your browser, operating system and screen size, and each run's score with your device's name (its graphics chip, or for an iPhone its screen size).

    There's no account, no player ID kept on your device, no ads and no tracking across sites. The leaderboard shows your device's name and score, never who you are.
