Build 17: tutorial levels, Skip into Quick Play, Shader Baker, Glitchless 0.5.1, light look, try cards

- Tutorial: each lesson and item is its own Glitchless level (tutorial_1_up_and_down ... tutorial_8_prism), left on Skip; tutorial_skip says which lesson. A Skip on the first run goes straight into Quick Play instead of Home.

- Shader Baker on in the iOS, Android and Google Play presets. First frame on a fresh install: iPhone 16 Pro Max 5.25 s to 1.79 s, Galaxy A15 5.56 s to 4.97 s.

- Light look for slow GPUs: the sky at half size, its stars drawn apart as quads, no glow. It switches on when the median frame in the first seconds is over 24 ms. Galaxy A15 Home: 17 fps to 53 fps.

- Home's try card: the Voyage card stays until the Voyage is played.
